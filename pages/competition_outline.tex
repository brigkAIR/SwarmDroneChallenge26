\section{Competition Rules}

\subsection{Playing Field}
The playing field is a rectangular area with the following dimensions (Figure~\ref{fig:playing-field}):
\begin{itemize}
	\item{Length: 20 meters}
	\item{Width: 10 meters}
	\item{Height: 6 meters}
\end{itemize}

% Abbildung der Ziel-Box (innerhalb der Section)
The playing field is divided into three zones:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{Two Team Zones (red and blue): The home base of each team, where the drones start, land and targets are strategically located.}
	\item{One Neutral Zone in the middle: A neutral territory for strategic maneuvers.}
\end{itemize}
The sizes shown in the graphic are approximate values. The exact dimensions may vary slightly.

\subsection{Target Objects}
The objective of the game is to change the color of the target objects to your team's color (red or blue). All gameplay mechanics, scoring rules, and special conditions are described in detail in Section~\ref{sec:game-logic}.
\begin{itemize}
	\item{Each team zone has three target objects, for a total of 6 in the playing field.}
	\item{These objects change their color based on their interaction with the drones:}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item{Default state: Matches the color of the team zone.}
		\item{Triggered state: When an enemy drone approaches and targets the box, it changes to the attacking team's color, updates the ArUco marker, and plays a horn signal. A subsequent enemy drone from the opposing team can then trigger it again.}
	\end{itemize}
	\item{Dimensions of each target box:}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item{Height: \SI{55}{\centi\meter} (open: \SI{73}{\centi\meter}), Width: \SI{57.5}{\centi\meter}, Depth: \SI{37.5}{\centi\meter}}
	\end{itemize}
	\item{Detection: The target boxes detect approaching drones via an internal RFID tag attached to the drone. The system can reliably identify drones within a height range of approximately 1--2 meters.}

\caption{Example of an SDC target box.}
\label{fig:target-box}
\end{figure}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Gameplay}
\label{sec:gameplay}
\begin{itemize}
	\item{Teams: 2--5 drones per team (independent team sizes), ready to battle!}
	\item{Start Setup: Drones may be placed inside their designated team zone and powered on within the cage.}
	\item{Cage Clearance: Once all human operators and staff have exited the cage and the area is confirmed clear, the cage is closed.}
	\item{Final Start Signal: After the cage is secured, the final start signal is given and all drones are permitted to take off and begin flight operations.}
	\item{Action: Drones fly around the arena, trigger target objects to change color, and score game points for their team. Full scoring rules are defined in Section~\ref{sec:game-logic}.}
\item{Defense Strategy:
	Teams may defend target boxes by positioning drones nearby.
	Strictly prohibited: physical obstruction, continuous hovering over targets, capture system interference, and all forms of \textbf{jamming or electronic warfare}.
	\textbf{"Sitting dead duck" behavior} — that is, passive sitting or hovering directly above the boxes — is not allowed and will lead to \textbf{disqualification}.}
	\item{Time Limit: The game lasts for a maximum of 10 minutes. The team with the most game points at the end wins the match!}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Game Logic}
\label{sec:game-logic}

\subsubsection{Goal}
Score \textbf{game points} by conquering enemy boxes and finish the match with more game points than the other team, or win instantly with a Special Maneuver.


\subsubsection{ArUco Marker Encoding}
Each physical box displays an ArUco marker from the \texttt{DICT\_4X4\_50} dictionary. The marker ID encodes the current state of the box:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{\texttt{4x} — box is currently \textbf{red}.}
	\item{\texttt{3x} — box is currently \textbf{blue}.}
	\item{The second digit is the \textbf{box ID} (1--6).}
\end{itemize}

\hline
\textbf{Box ID} & \textbf{Color} & \textbf{Marker ID} \\
\hline
1 & red  & 41 \\
2 & red  & 42 \\
3 & red  & 43 \\
4 & blue & 34 \\
5 & blue & 35 \\
6 & blue & 36 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\label{tab:aruco-ids}

When a box is captured, the displayed marker changes with the box color. Example: box \texttt{3} starts red (marker \texttt{43}), is captured by blue (changes to \texttt{33}), then recaptured by red (changes back to \texttt{43}).

\subsubsection{Capturing a Box}
An enemy box changes to your team's color if:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{your drone hovers over it for at least \texttt{2 seconds}, and}
	\item{no defending drone is detected in that box.}
\end{itemize}

A home-zone box changes back to your team's color immediately when recaptured.

After a successful capture, the box is locked for \texttt{5 seconds}.

\paragraph{1 Game Point}
You get \texttt{1 game point} if:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{your team captures an enemy box,}
	\item{the capturing drone returns home, and}
	\item{this happens before that box is recaptured by the other team.}
\end{itemize}

If the box is recaptured first, you still get \texttt{1 game point} only if the capturing drone had already reached home; otherwise you get \texttt{0 game points}.

\paragraph{5 Game Points}
You get \texttt{5 game points} if:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{your team captures an enemy box,}
	\item{your team started the game with at least \texttt{2} drones,}
	\item{at the moment of capture, all drones of your team are \textbf{outside} your home zone, and}
	\item{then all your drones return home before that box is recaptured.}
\end{itemize}

If one of your drones was still in the home zone at the moment of capture, \texttt{5 game points} are not possible for that attempt. If the box is recaptured before all drones return, \texttt{5 game points} are no longer possible, but you may still get \texttt{1 game point} if the capturing drone had already reached home.

\paragraph{10 Game Points}
You get \texttt{10 game points} if:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{two different drones of your team capture two different enemy boxes,}
	\item{both captures occur within less than \texttt{1 second} of each other, and}
	\item{your team started the game with at least \texttt{2} drones.}
\end{itemize}

If the timing is too slow, there is no \texttt{10-point} capture and both captures are treated separately (each can still become \texttt{1} or \texttt{5} game points). If three boxes are captured almost simultaneously, one valid pair may yield \texttt{10 game points} while the third capture is treated separately.

\paragraph{Home Zone Recapture}
If your team recaptures a box in your own home zone:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{the box reverts to your team's color immediately, and}
	\item{you receive \texttt{0 game points}.}
\end{itemize}

This rule prevents defensive game point farming.

\paragraph{Special Maneuver}
If one team controls all \texttt{6} boxes continuously for at least \texttt{5 seconds}, that team wins the match immediately.

After the instant win is triggered, a final \texttt{10-second} resolution window remains during which already-open scoring attempts may still finish, but the winning team does not change.

\subsubsection{Additional Rules}
\begin{itemize}
	\item{One drone can only run one scoring attempt at a time.}
	\item{A drone may only start a new attempt after it has been detected back in its own home zone.}
	\item{One capture attempt can only score once: \texttt{1}, \texttt{5}, or \texttt{10} game points. These scores do not stack for the same attempt.}
	\item{Game point priority for the same capture event: Special Maneuver $>$ \texttt{10} $>$ \texttt{5} $>$ \texttt{1}.}
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Tournament}
All matches are played head-to-head per the rules in Section~\ref{sec:gameplay}. The tournament is played by \textbf{6 teams} in three stages:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{\textbf{Opening Round} --- All 6 teams compete. The \textbf{4 best-ranked} teams advance to the semi-finals.}
	\item{\textbf{Semi-Finals} --- The 4 remaining teams play 2 matches. The \textbf{2 winners} advance to the final.}
	\item{\textbf{Final} --- The 2 remaining teams compete for the championship.}
\end{itemize}
Match pairings are determined by the organizers and announced via Discord. In the event that an assigned opponent cannot participate, a replacement team is drawn by lot or, if unavailable, the match is scored as a technical win for the other team.

\subsection{Winning the Challenge}
\label{sec:winning}
The overall winner is the team with the most \textbf{challenge points} at the end of the tournament. Challenge points are accumulated from three categories, with a maximum of \textbf{25 challenge points} total:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\hline
\textbf{Category} & \textbf{Max.\ Challenge Points} \\
\hline
Match wins (5 challenge points per won match) & 15 \\
Jury evaluation (pitch \& overall approach) & 10 \\
Technical report & 5 \\
\hline
\textbf{Total} & \textbf{25} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Overall challenge scoring overview.}
\end{table}

\subsubsection{Match Performance}
Each won match awards the winning team \textbf{5 challenge points}. A team can win at most one match per stage (Opening Round, Semi-Final, Final), for a maximum of \textbf{15 challenge points} from match performance.

\subsubsection{Jury Evaluation}
A jury of experts awards up to \textbf{10 challenge points} based on the team's pitch presentation and overall technical approach demonstrated during the competition.

\subsubsection{Technical Report}
Each team must submit a technical report that describes their solution. The report is evaluated by the jury for up to \textbf{5 challenge points} and must address the following:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{\textbf{Localisation} --- describe the localisation approach in detail, e.g.\ Kalman filter state definition, sensor fusion, or other state estimation methods.}
	\item{\textbf{Flight Control \& Trajectory Planning} --- explain the flight controller design and trajectory planning strategies used.}
	\item{\textbf{Swarm Strategy} --- describe how the drones coordinate and cooperate as a swarm to achieve the mission objective.}
\end{itemize}
Teams that open-source their code will receive a positive consideration in the technical report evaluation.

The technical report is due \textbf{4 days before the final}.
